# Conditional follow-up roadmap — proposals only

1. Recover campaign evidence before compute. Hypothesis: pilot effects replicate across independent seeds. Obtain all run records/configs/history/artifacts and crash logs; distinguish unique conditions, restarts and pilot records. Compare architecture differences within environment with paired-seed resampling only when pairing is justified. No training compute. Success is auditable coverage and reproducible seed-level contrasts; missing conditions remain explicit.
2. Diagnose transient fitting using saved pools/parameters if available. Compare original AdamW with learning-rate factors 0.25, 0.5, 1 at fixed initialization, teacher, targets and minibatch order. Select dog small/medium/large and myo medium/large, at initial/middle/final checks, eight independent parent seeds and five paired probe rounds. Record intermediate full-pool MSE, centered output variance, gradients, update norms, nonfinites and optimizer moments. Optimizer overshoot predicts reduced fixed-pool spikes at lower rates; pure sampling noise predicts minibatch-only spikes. Parent-seed paired contrasts; fitting time must be benchmarked before a compute estimate. Nominal maximum 5 cells × 8 seeds × 3 checks × 5 rounds × 3 rates × 2 arms × 2000 = 7.2 million fit updates. This is diagnostic compute requiring separate authorization, even on CPU.
3. Distinguish finite-budget difficulty from persistent adaptation loss. Same saved tasks and critics; budgets 1000, 2000, 4000, 8000 with continuous optimizer state and shared prefixes. Compare held-out error as well as fitting error; matching current/fresh convergence supports a budget explanation, persistent gaps motivate further tests. Eight parent seeds per selected cell, controls as above. Extra updates per paired trajectory relative to 2000 are 6000; runtime depends on measured per-update throughput. No pretense that longer-budget results are original-protocol scores.
4. Test critic-to-actor propagation only after replication. Fork saved states before independently defined pathology onset; randomize an approved critic intervention versus matched identity control, preserve actor/replay/RNG and continue for a prespecified horizon. Owner must select intervention and scientific thresholds. Use at least eight paired parent seeds in a replicated affected cell plus an unaffected cell. Record return episodes, Q bias against rollout estimates, policy KL, action gradients, entropy, TD residuals and adaptation. A successful manipulation must change the critic phenotype before an actor contrast is interpretable. Compute is two arms × selected cells × seeds × continuation horizon divided by measured throughput; unavailable timings prevent a defensible GPU-hour number.

No experiments launched. These proposals require owner decisions and do not amend the methodology.
